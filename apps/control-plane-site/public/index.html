Connect your devices with Tailscale.

Tailscale puts your Mac and iPhone on a private network, so the app can reach your Mac from anywhere without exposing it to the internet.

  1. 1Install Tailscale on your Mac and iPhone and sign in to the same account.
  2. 2In the Tailscale admin console, turn on MagicDNS and HTTPS certificates under DNS.
  3. 3In Control Plane on your Mac, open Settings → Connections and turn on Tailscale HTTPS.
  4. 4Create a pairing link on the same page and scan its QR code with the Control Plane iPhone app.
