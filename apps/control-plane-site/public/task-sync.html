Task Sync.

Your Control Plane tasks, the same on your Mac and your iPhone.

Task Sync is $5 a month plus applicable tax. Cancel anytime; it stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Turn it on

  1. Open Control Plane on your Mac, go to Settings > Task Sync and sign in with your email.
  2. Choose Subscribe and finish checkout in your browser.
  3. Sign in with the same email on your iPhone. Your tasks sync on their own from then on.

Manage or cancel

In Settings > Task Sync on your Mac, choose Manage subscription to update your card, download invoices, or cancel. If you got here from that page, you're done and can close this tab.

If a payment fails

Sync keeps working for 7 days while we retry your card. Update your card from Settings > Task Sync > Manage subscription to keep it on. Your tasks stay on your devices either way.

Questions

Email support@lucive.app. Control Plane itself stays free; Task Sync is the only paid part, and charges from your AI providers are separate.
